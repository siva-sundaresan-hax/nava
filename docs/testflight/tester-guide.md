# Nava — TestFlight Tester Guide (Phase 1 build)

**Thank you for testing Nava.** You're one of about 10 people trying the first playable build. It's rough on purpose: the cards are plain placeholders and the art comes later. What we need from you is whether the *game* is fun.

**Time needed:** about 15 minutes for your first game, plus 5 minutes for the feedback form.

---

## 1. Install

1. Open the TestFlight invite email or link on your iPhone.
2. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store if you don't have it.
3. Tap **Accept**, then **Install** for Nava.
4. Builds expire after 90 days. If a new build arrives, TestFlight will tell you to update.

## 2. The game in one minute

> **Fate deals; you play the chart.**

- The screen shows a ring of **12 rashi fields** (the zodiac signs), colored by element: fire, earth, air, water.
- **5 graha cards** (planets) are dealt face-down onto random fields.
- Each turn you **flip one** card, then choose one move:
  - **Place** — leave it where it was dealt.
  - **Move** — shift it up to 2 fields. The first step is free; the second costs 1 point.
  - **Exchange** — swap it with a graha you already placed.
- You also hold **2 nakshatra special cards**. Each one is a one-time power. Use it whenever you like.
- After 5 turns the game ends. You get a **harmony score** and a three-line **Reading** of your chart.

**Scoring quick reference**

| What | Points |
|---|---|
| Graha in its exalted sign / own sign / friend's sign / neutral sign | +10 / +6 / +4 / +2 |
| Graha in an enemy's sign / debilitated | 0 / −6 |
| Two friendly grahas side by side | +4 |
| Two enemy grahas side by side | −4 |
| Guru next to anyone | +2 |
| All three signs of one element occupied | +9 |
| 3+ of the four angular signs occupied (Mesha, Karka, Tula, Makara) | +6 |
| No graha in an enemy sign or debilitated | +5 |

Rahu **doubles** his field's score, good or bad. Ketu's field scores **0**, but he cancels your worst enemy link.

**Score bands:** 50+ Yoga · 38–49 Harmonious · 25–37 Working chart · 12–24 Difficult · under 12, "the stars were not consulted properly."

You don't need to know astrology. The score updates as you play, so you can learn by experimenting.

## 3. What to try

Please play in this order:

1. **Free Play.** Play one game without overthinking it. Note anything confusing.
2. **Free Play again.** Only if you want to. *We'll ask whether you did, and we won't judge either way.* This is the most important thing we're measuring.
3. **Daily Deal.** Everyone gets the same deal today. Write down your score so we can compare players.
4. **Contest Deal.** This is the fixed deal for the future weekly contest. Try to get your best score.

While you play, pay attention to:

- Was there a real choice each turn, or was the best move obvious?
- Did a chart that *looked* harmonious also *score* well?
- Which special cards did you use? Which ones did you never use?
- Did the Reading make you laugh, nod or disagree?
- How long did each game take?

## 4. Reporting problems

- **Crash or bug:** take a screenshot, then use TestFlight's **Send Beta Feedback**. Or just take a screenshot in Nava and TestFlight will offer to send it. Tell us what you tapped right before it happened.
- **Everything else:** fill in the feedback form (`feedback-form.md`, or the link we sent you).

## 5. Good to know

- Nava is a **strategy card game set in the world of Indian astrology**. It's for entertainment and does not predict anything.
- The build collects no accounts and no personal data. Saves stay on your phone.
- Please don't share screenshots publicly yet. Sharing with family is fine.
